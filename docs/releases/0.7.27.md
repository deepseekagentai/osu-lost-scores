The first release of the Rust rewrite. It finds your osu!stable plays that ScoreV1 overwrote and the pp they were worth.

## What it does

- Reads your replays (`Data/r`) and your Songs folder, calculates pp with the live osu! formula, and lists the plays that a later play with a higher score replaced on the server. The cut-off is 9 October 2024, when osu!stable stopped deleting overwritten bests.
- Shows the Potential Top: your top 200 as it would be with those plays kept, with the pp and accuracy it would give, and your Online Top next to it.
- After every scan it saves three pictures for sharing: a summary badge, Lost Scores and Potential Top. A click opens a picture, a right click copies it, and one button uploads all three to catbox.moe and copies the links.
- Keeps every scan in a history that you can reopen. Old scans go to the system trash from the scan list.

## Downloads

- Windows 10 and 11: `lost-scores-0.7.27-windows-x64.exe`. One file, no installation: run it from anywhere.
- Linux: `lost-scores-0.7.27-linux-x64.tar.gz`, for glibc 2.35 or newer, with `install.sh` for `~/.local/bin`, the launcher entry and the icons.
- macOS 11 or newer on Apple Silicon: `lost-scores-0.7.27-macos-arm64.zip` with Lost Scores.app.
- The command line for Windows and macOS: `lost-scores-cli-0.7.27-windows-x64.zip` and `lost-scores-cli-0.7.27-macos-arm64.zip`. On Linux it is in the archive.
- `SHA256SUMS` has the checksums.

The builds are not signed yet. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn on the first start: choose "More info", then "Run anyway". On macOS, the system says it cannot verify the app: choose Done, then "Open Anyway" in System Settings, Privacy & Security.

## What you need

- osu!stable with your replays. The program only reads the osu! folder and never changes it.
- For now, the keys of your own osu! OAuth application, for the map statuses and your online top. Create one at https://osu.ppy.sh/home/account/edit and choose "Use Custom API Keys". A mode without your own keys needs the project's server, which comes later.

## Notes

- pp comes from a fork of rosu-pp that follows the live formula (osu! 2026.1026.0). Checked against the server's pp for a full top 200: 199 of 200 within 0.01 pp.
- An upload to catbox.moe is public and anonymous. It cannot be deleted for free, so the program asks before the first upload.
