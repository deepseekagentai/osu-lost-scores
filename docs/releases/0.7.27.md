The first release of the Rust rewrite. It finds your osu!stable plays that ScoreV1 overwrote and the pp they were worth.

## What it does

- Reads your replays (`Data/r`) and your Songs folder, calculates pp with the live osu! formula, and lists the plays the server dropped: those a later play with a higher score replaced, and those it never kept because an earlier play on the map had a higher score. The cut-off is 9 October 2024, when osu!stable stopped deleting overwritten bests.
- Shows the Potential Top: your top 200 as it would be with those plays kept, with the pp and accuracy it would give, and your Online Top next to it.
- After every scan it saves three pictures for sharing: a summary badge, Lost Scores and Potential Top. A click opens a picture, a right click offers Copy image, and one button uploads all three to catbox.moe and copies the links.
- Keeps every scan in a history that you can reopen. Old scans go to the system trash from the scan list.
- With the osu! login, every scan is also saved, privately, to your profile on https://lost.lemon4ik.kz. Share publishes a scan to the Hall of Copium, and "Share N replays too" adds the replays of the lost plays in your Potential Top. The site checks published scans against osu!, follows which of your lost plays you have fixed since, and gives medals for the fixes.

## Downloads

- Windows 10 and 11: `lost-scores-0.7.27-windows-x64.exe`. One file, no installation: run it from anywhere.
- Linux: `lost-scores-0.7.27-linux-x64.tar.gz`, for glibc 2.35 or newer, with `install.sh` for `~/.local/bin`, the launcher entry and the icons.
- macOS 11 or newer on Apple Silicon: `lost-scores-0.7.27-macos-arm64.zip` with Lost Scores.app.
- The command line for Windows and macOS: `lost-scores-cli-0.7.27-windows-x64.zip` and `lost-scores-cli-0.7.27-macos-arm64.zip`. On Linux it is in the archive.
- `SHA256SUMS` has the checksums.

The builds are not signed yet. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn on the first start: choose "More info", then "Run anyway". With Smart App Control turned on in Windows 11, an unsigned program cannot start at all until the builds are signed. On macOS 15 and newer, the system says it cannot verify the app: choose Done, then "Open Anyway" in System Settings, Privacy & Security. On macOS 14 and older, Control-click the app and choose Open.

## What you need

- osu!stable with your replays. The program only reads the osu! folder and never changes it.
- An osu! account to log in with. Instead of logging in, you can use the keys of your own osu! OAuth application: create one at https://osu.ppy.sh/home/account/edit and choose "Use Custom API Keys". With your own keys the program asks osu! directly and keeps everything on your computer, but it cannot save or publish scans.

## Notes

- pp comes from a fork of rosu-pp that follows the live formula (osu! 2026.1026.0). Checked against the server's pp for a full top 200: 199 of 200 within 0.01 pp.
- Logging in accepts the terms of use (https://lost.lemon4ik.kz/terms) and the privacy policy (https://lost.lemon4ik.kz/privacy).
- The window checks for a new version once a day and from About. It installs only files signed with the project's key and uses them after a restart.
- An upload to catbox.moe is public and anonymous. It cannot be deleted for free, so the program asks before the first upload.
